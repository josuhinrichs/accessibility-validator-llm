<section class="howToFix">
<h2>How to Fix the Problem</h2>
<div class="howToFixData">
<ul>
<li>
    The <code>aria-braillelabel</code> or
    <code>aria-brailleroledescription</code> attribute may have been placed on
    the wrong element, such as a parent or child of the correct element. The
    attribute should be put on a different element.
  </li>
<li>
    The element with <code>aria-braillelabel</code> attribute needs an
    <code>aria-label</code>
    attribute or other attribute that gives it an accessible name.
  </li>
<li>
    The element with <code>aria-brailleroledescription</code> attribute needs a
    <code>aria-roledescription</code> attribute.
  </li>
<li>
    The <code>aria-braillelabel</code> or
    <code>aria-brailleroledescription</code> attribute serves no function and
    should be removed.
  </li>
</ul>

</section><section class="whyImportant">
<h2>Why it Matters</h2>
<div class="howToFixData">
<p>
  ARIA braille attributes were introduced to allow adjusting how labels and role
  descriptions are rendered on a braille display. They cannot be the only
  attribute providing a label, or a role description. When used without a
  corresponding label or role description ARIA says to ignore these attributes,
  although this may not happen consistently in screen readers and other
  assistive technologies.
</p>
</div>
</section><section class="description">
<h2>Rule Description</h2>
<div class="howToFixData">
<p>
  WAI-ARIA requires that the <code>aria-braillelabel</code> attribute is only
  ever used on elements with an accessible name, such as from
  <code>aria-label</code>. Similarly,
  <code>aria-brailleroledescription</code> is required to only ever be used on
  elements with <code>aria-roledescription</code>.
</p>
</div>
</section><section class="algorithm">
<h2>The Algorithm (in simple terms)</h2>
<div class="howToFixData">
<p>
  Checks that <code>aria-braillelabel</code> is only used on elements with a
  non-empty label, and that <code>aria-brailleroledescription</code> is only
  used on elements with a non-empty <code>aria-roledescription</code>.
</p>
</div>
</section>
