Before we talk features...

Our non-negotiables

Canvas is continuously evolving, but we're steadfast on certain things.

Equitable, accessible learning

With a strong commitment to accessibility standards and inclusive design at its core, Canvas helps institutions meet diverse learner needs.

Community-powered innovation

Through regular feedback loops with a global community of educators and technologists, Canvas continues to grow with and for the people who use it.

Flexible, scalable integration

Built in the cloud on open technology, Canvas integrates with 1000+ external tools, giving educators the freedom to compose their ideal teaching and learning environments.

Ready for a flyover? See 60 seconds' worth of key Canvas features.

Teaching-centered, learning-driven design

The difference-makers. The game-changers. The force-multipliers.

  • Simple educator workflows
  • Administrative efficiencies
  • Data and insights
  • Free mobile apps
Simple educator workflows
  • SpeedGrader
  • Message Students Who...
  • Gradebook

SpeedGrader

Grade student submissions, leave comments, and annotate documents, all in one place. SpeedGrader makes it easier to give critical, contextual feedback and interact productively with students.

Message Students Who...

Connect with students based on instructor-defined criteria, and deliver support when and where it's needed.

Gradebook

More than just marks, Gradebook offers a clear view of learner progress. Educators can enter marks, manage assignments, adjust weightings, and customise how they view and organise data.

Administrative efficiencies
  • Blueprint courses
  • Customizable feature settings

Blueprint courses

Scale engaging curriculum across your school, district, or institution, and push course updates with a single click. Great instructional design and centrally managed courses just got infinitely easier to deploy.

Customizable feature settings

Create secure and managed teaching and learning spaces that adapt to different user groups and reflect your institution's branding, hierarchy, and roles.

Data and insights
  • Canvas Data
  • In-app reporting

Canvas Data

Get access to raw, event-level data files—ideal for building customised dashboards, running advanced queries, and integrating with your institution’s data warehouse.

In-app reporting

Get real-time visibility into course activity, student progress, and engagement trends—without ever leaving Canvas.

Free mobile apps
  • Student
  • Teacher
  • Parent

Student

Learners can stay up to date, on the go, on any device.

  • View grades and course content
  • Submit assignments
  • Use to-do lists and calendars
  • Send and receive messages
  • Post to discussions
  • Watch videos
  • Take quizzes

Teacher

Educators can facilitate courses from anywhere.

  • Browse submissions and provide feedback with the app-embedded mobile SpeedGrader.
  • Post announcements, send messages, and participate in course discussions.
  • Change due dates, publish assignments, and edit course content.

Parent

In the Schools Canvas world, parents get at-a-glance visibility into their child's education.

  • View assignment descriptions and due dates
  • View course announcements
  • Set reminders for assignments
  • See grades for assignments and courses

Canvas Studio

Canvas Studio is the two-way video platform that turns passive watching into active learning.

Canvas Studio is built for:

  • seamless video creation and sharing
  • interactive discussions inside the video timeline
  • actionable analytics to track engagement
  • integration across courses, devices, and the Canvas LMS

Canvas Catalog

Canvas Catalog allows you to promote, centralize, and brand your continuing education and professional learning programs in an online marketplace.

Canvas Catalog is built for:

  • discoverable course listings and programs
  • seamless enrollment and payment workflows
  • professional, continuing, and vocational learning
  • custom branding and configuration
