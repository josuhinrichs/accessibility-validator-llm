More Than the Book

Tools to learn more effectively.

  1. Learn better by listening? Use ‘Read Aloud’ and set a speed that suits you, up to a chipmunk-friendly 2x.

  2. Quit flicking back-and-forth. Search for keywords in the book and cut a chunk off your reading time.

  3. Keep all your highlights, notes, quotes and citations in your Notebook, and return to them any time – even without an active membership.

  4. Use BeeLine Reader’s colour-changing font to pull your eyes through text, helping you read at twice your normal speed.
