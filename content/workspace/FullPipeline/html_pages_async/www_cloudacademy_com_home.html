Need to know

Frequently asked questions

What’s the main difference between the personal and enterprise plans?

The enterprise plan is designed for organisations looking to learn, master and apply new skills for organisational change across digital skills from Cloud Platforms, AI & Machine Learning to Digital Marketing and more. Individual users have access to QA’s technical library of training only including Cloud Platforms, IT Operations and Software Development. This also includes hands-on labs, and interface.


Plus, enterprise team managers and administrators have the ability to:

  • Manage training due dates by assigning courses and individual lessons. Optionally assign assessments as well, to better understand the staff’s baseline skills.
  • Automate training reminders and programme personalisation. Use optional nudges to remind staff who have fallen behind on progress. And use AI-driven programs to dynamically assign the training content needed for proficiency – skipping past content that staff have already mastered.
  • Stay on top of key metrics, certifications, and skill attainment. Powerful analytics track what’s most important to you and your organisation.
  • Intelligently assign projects to qualified staff by using our granular skill analytics and leaderboard to find top performers in each skill area.

How will QA help my business?

Tech leaders choose QA to go beyond training with skills readiness. Companies can upskill, reskill, and certify their tech teams at scale through personalized, AI-driven courses and hands-on labs – with precision analytics that help leaders intelligently assign projects to qualified staff. We provide everything from fundamental learning to advanced scenario training on the multi-cloud (AWS, Azure, GCP, Alibaba), operating between clouds (containers, DevOps, privacy, security), and beyond (AI/ML, data science, IoT, software development). Tech teams meet client milestones, unlock innovation accelerators, drive additional business, and improve profitability by upskilling on QA.

All learning content, hands-on labs, AI assistant access, and certification prep – with no hidden fees.

Can I manage learning across a team?

Yes. Team and enterprise plans include tools for progress tracking, skill assessment, and management reporting.

Do you offer integrations?

Absolutely. Enterprise plans include SSO, LMS integrations, custom APIs, and more.

Finance, telecom, healthcare, retail, government, and more – any team that depends on digital skills.
