Our Approach

Knovva takes a design thinking and human centered approach to all our work. We focus on the values, culture, voice, and context of our partners; and then we collaborate to generate solutions that unlock human capacity.

Our team, composed of social scientists, educators, instructional designers, and clinicians work alongside organizational leaders, educators, and policymakers throughout the United States and around the world to leverage the power of learning to foster growth and promote impact.
