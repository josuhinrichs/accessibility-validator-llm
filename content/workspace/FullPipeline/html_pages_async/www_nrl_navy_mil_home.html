Compact Coronagraph: The Future of Space Weather

Take a look into NRL's Compact Coronagraph (CCOR)! Developed for NOAA, CCOR revolutionizes space weather forecasting. This compact telescope spots solar storms that can threaten our communication tech. CCOR, with NASA, delivers real-time images, giving crucial warning time.
